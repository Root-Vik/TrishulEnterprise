About Trishul Enterprise

Engineered with
Precision Since 1985

Founded as Trishul Engineering Works and reestablished as Trishul Enterprise, we have been at the forefront of submersible pump component manufacturing for over four decades, operating from the heart of Gujarat's industrial corridor in Odhav, Ahmedabad.

We manufacture technically advanced, energy-efficient, and lifelong-durable spare parts for submersible pump sets, submersible motors, and openwell pumps — trusted by engineers and distributors across India and internationally.

  • Tilting pad thrust bearing technology for maximum hydrodynamic load capacity
  • Proprietary CARBOTEFF bearing material — superior lubrication, lightweight, economical
  • CNC-machined impellers for precise hydraulic alignment and optimal performance
  • 5,000–10,000 unit monthly capacity with 10–20 day dispatch
  • IEC registered exporter serving domestic and international markets
